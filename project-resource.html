Confirmed brief

Make a small timber box with a lid

Obtain timber from the teacher and calculate the stock required from the box sides, top and bottom. Hardware is teacher supplied or teacher approved; students are not required to source hinges because colour, size, price and profile errors are common.

High-level production pathway

Follow the approved sequence and teacher demonstrations

  1. Measure, mark and square-cut the required components.
  2. Prepare the dovetail joints.
  3. Rebate the back.
  4. Make the finger joint and rebate the bottom.
  5. Dry-check, glue and clamp the box square.
  6. Prepare the lid and its chamfers.
  7. Fit the teacher-approved small hinges.

This pathway names the confirmed order only. It does not provide dimensions, tolerances, router settings, machine setup, cutting directions or permission to begin practical work.

Workshop control

Teacher direction remains the practical authority

Current school procedures, SOPs, risk controls and teacher demonstrations control hand-saw, chisel, marking-gauge, router-table, finger-jointer, clamp, hand-plane and mallet use. Stop and ask whenever the plan, stock, hardware, setup or next action is uncertain.
