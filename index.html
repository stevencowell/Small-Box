The course flow

A predictable routine for each module

1. Read

Read each section in the order shown and use the provided references.

2. Practise

Complete guided checks to verify safety and accuracy before the next step.

3. Capture

Use scaffolded responses to write what you checked and what you will improve.

4. Save

Download each module PDF before moving on or leaving the site.

Student evidence: All data is saved locally in this browser. Keep a PDF backup for school submission and your own reference.

Read the Stage 5 outcome list
  1. IND5-1: identifies, assesses and manages risks and WHS issues with tools, equipment, materials and procedures.
  2. IND5-2: applies design principles in the design and manufacture process.
  3. IND5-3: selects and uses tools, equipment and processes to produce quality projects.
  4. IND5-4: selects and uses suitable materials and technologies.
  5. IND5-5: uses effective communication in planning, production and presentation.
  6. IND5-6: participates responsibly in collaborative workshop work.
  7. IND5-7: transfers techniques to new problems.
  8. IND5-8: evaluates project quality for function, aesthetics and sustainability.
  9. IND5-9: analyses timber technologies and workshop methods.
  10. IND5-10: considers social, environmental and economic impacts of timber technology.
