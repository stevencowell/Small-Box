Weeks 5-6: Complete, finish and evaluate

Complete the confirmed lid and hinge stage, then close the project with controlled finishing and evidence-based evaluation.

Theory 1

Lid chamfers and hinge-fit checks

The approved sequence prepares chamfers on the lid and fits small hinges that are supplied or approved by the teacher. A chamfer is a bevelled edge. The exact chamfer dimensions, hinge position and fitting method remain controlled by the approved project source and teacher demonstration.

Before final fitting, check that the lid aligns with the box, moves as intended and does not rely on forcing. Confirm that the hardware is suitable for the project and that the required teacher check has occurred.

  • Check lid alignment and movement.
  • Confirm hinge suitability and approval.
  • Use only the demonstrated fitting process.
  • Record the checks before and after final fitting.
Key idea: Hardware fitting is a functional and authority-controlled stage, not a decorative afterthought.

Surface readiness and finish controls

A surface is ready when it is clean, dry and even, with no unresolved defects that require correction. One smooth area does not prove the whole box is ready.

The teacher, current school SOP and approved product information control the exact finish and application process. Required PPE, ventilation and handling controls remain active during finishing.

  • Inspect the full surface and correct unresolved issues.
  • Confirm the approved product and process.
  • Record observations between checks.
  • Allow the approved process to control handling and curing.

Final quality and evidence review

The final review should include lid movement, hinge security, joint condition, box stability, surface condition and finish quality. Record what was observed rather than relying on general claims.

A PMI review identifies a supported Plus, an honest Minus and an Interesting question or next improvement. A remaining limitation should be stated clearly with a realistic way to improve it in a future project.

Key idea: Strong evaluation connects the approved brief, observable evidence and a realistic next action.
