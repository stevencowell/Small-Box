Confirmed authority

Preserve the approved brief and production order

Students make a small box with an optional lid. Record the teacher-confirmed open or lidded choice. Students receive timber from the teacher and calculate stock from the sides and bottom, plus the top/lid only if selected. Hardware is teacher supplied or approved only where required. The confirmed order is marking and square cutting; dovetails; back rebate; finger joint; bottom rebate; and square glue-up. Lid chamfers and small hinges apply only to the relevant lidded version. For an open box, collect opening, edge and access evidence instead.

Dimensions, stock sizes, tolerances, settings, machine setup, assessment conditions and permission to begin remain teacher controlled.
