The existing three modules do not match the approved project source

The current lesson data describes a divider-and-butt-joint box. The approved Small Box source describes a lidded box using dovetails, rebates, a finger joint, chamfers and small hinges. Do not treat the existing module production wording or divider-based image as approved technical authority.

Confirmed authority

Preserve the approved brief and production order

Students make a small box with a lid, receive timber from the teacher and calculate stock from the sides, top and bottom. Hardware is teacher supplied or approved. The confirmed order is marking and square cutting; dovetails; back rebate; finger joint; bottom rebate; square glue-up; lid chamfers; and small hinges.

Dimensions, stock sizes, tolerances, settings, machine setup, assessment conditions and permission to begin remain teacher controlled.
