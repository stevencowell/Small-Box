Program Overview

This Stage 5 Small Box unit follows the approved high-level pathway for a lidded timber box. Students connect source checking, workshop controls, accurate marking, confirmed joinery, square assembly, lid fitting, finishing and evaluation.

  1. Weeks 1–2 — Brief, sources and safe preparation: Confirm the project brief, identify teacher-controlled technical information, inspect material and establish marking references.
  2. Weeks 3–4 — Approved joinery and square assembly: Study the purpose and sequence of the dovetails, back rebate, finger joint and bottom rebate; record dry-fit, correction and square glue-up checks.
  3. Weeks 5–6 — Lid, hinges, finish and evaluation: Check the lid chamfers and teacher-approved hinges, prepare surfaces, apply the authorised finish and evaluate the final evidence.

Dimensions, stock sizes, tolerances, machine settings, exact practical procedures, assessment conditions and permission to begin remain controlled by the teacher and approved local sources.
