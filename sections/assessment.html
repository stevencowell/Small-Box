Assessment

The teacher-issued assessment task is the authority for marks, weighting, dates, adjustments, submission requirements and conditions. The points below support evidence preparation but do not replace that task.

  • Safe workshop practice: Record how hazards, permission points and teacher directions were addressed.
  • Quality of construction: Provide evidence of source checking, accurate marking, approved joinery, dry fitting, square assembly, lid and hinge checks, surface readiness and finish quality.
  • Theory and folio evidence: Complete the required module questions and keep PDF or folio backups as directed.
  • Reflection and evaluation: Use evidence to explain decisions, corrections, strengths and worthwhile improvements.

Browser data is a working copy and is not submitted automatically. Follow the teacher-approved local submission process.
