Advanced Activity: Source and Joinery Analysis

Extend your learning by comparing the confirmed construction features in the approved Small Box brief: dovetails, the back rebate, the finger joint, the bottom rebate, lid chamfers and small hinges.

  1. Explain how two confirmed features support the function, alignment or appearance of the finished box. Cite the approved source used.

  2. Choose one dry-fit or square-assembly check and explain what evidence would show that a correction was effective.
