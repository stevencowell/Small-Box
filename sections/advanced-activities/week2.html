Advanced Week 2: Adhesives & Design Improvements

Compare adhesive products using the teacher-provided label, technical information and safety data sheet. For PVA, polyurethane and epoxy products, investigate material compatibility, joint fit, water-resistance rating, working time, curing and safe handling. These details depend on the particular product. Expansion during curing does not remove the need for correct joint preparation: for example, the manufacturer's directions for Original Gorilla Glue require tight-fitting surfaces. This example is a source to compare, not approval to use that adhesive in class.

The Small Box lid is optional. Confirm an open or lidded version with the teacher; small hinges are teacher supplied or teacher approved only when the approved lid uses them. Compare a possible appearance or usability refinement with your chosen brief, explaining its benefit and any effect on materials, time or construction. For an open box, consider the opening, edge condition and access to the intended contents. A proposal does not authorise changing the approved joints, rebates or practical methods. Confirm the design choice and adhesive with the teacher before practical work.

Advanced Activities – Week 2 Tasks

  1. Explain the differences between PVA wood glue and polyurethane glue. When might you choose one over the other?

  2. Suggest one design modification that could improve the small box project. Explain how it would enhance the box’s function or appearance.
