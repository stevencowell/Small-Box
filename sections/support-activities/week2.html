Support Activity: Joinery, Dry Fit and Square Assembly

Use the approved project source to make a simple sequence map: dovetails, back rebate, finger joint, bottom rebate and square glue-up. Add the teacher-controlled check required before each practical stage.

  1. Name one approved joint or rebate and state its purpose in the box.

  2. Describe one dry-fit observation that should be recorded before square glue-up.

  3. Identify where the approved source or teacher direction is needed before practical work continues.
