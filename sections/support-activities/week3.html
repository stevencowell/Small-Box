Support Activity: Lid, Finish and Evaluation

Build an evidence checklist for the lid chamfers, teacher-approved hinges, surface readiness, authorised finish and final evaluation.

  1. What evidence would show that the lid and hinges are aligned and functioning as approved?

  2. What teacher-controlled information must be confirmed before surface preparation or finishing?

  3. Write one evidence-based strength and one worthwhile improvement for the finished box.
