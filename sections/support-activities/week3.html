Support Activity: Optional Lid, Finish and Evaluation

Build an evidence checklist for your approved open or lidded box, surface readiness, authorised finish and final evaluation. Lid chamfers and teacher-approved hinges are included only where applicable.

  1. For a selected lid, what evidence would show that it and any hinges align and function as approved? For an open box, what evidence would show suitable opening, edge condition and access to the intended contents?

  2. What teacher-controlled information must be confirmed before surface preparation or finishing?

  3. Write one evidence-based strength and one worthwhile improvement for the finished box.
