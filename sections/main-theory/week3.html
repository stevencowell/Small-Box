Main Theory — Weeks 5–6: Optional Lid, Finish and Evaluation

Optional lid or open-box checks

A lid is optional. For a selected lid, use the approved source to check the lid chamfers and, where fitted, teacher-supplied or teacher-approved small hinges. Record alignment, movement and any authorised correction. For an open box, record the approved lid-free choice and evidence of opening, edge and access checks instead.

Surface and finish readiness

Document how the surface was judged ready and how the authorised finish was controlled. Product choice, preparation, application, drying and disposal requirements remain teacher controlled.

Final evaluation

Evaluate the box against the approved brief. Link each claim about function, fit, construction quality and finish to a photograph, check, observation or correction record.
