Main Theory — Weeks 1–2: Brief, Sources and Safe Preparation

Approved brief and source authority

The project is a small timber box with a fitted lid. Use the teacher-issued brief and current local workshop documents for dimensions, stock sizes, tolerances, settings, practical methods and permission points.

Material and marking control

Check the supplied timber, identify the sides, top and bottom, and establish reliable reference faces and edges. Record the source used for every technical value before any irreversible work.

Workshop authority

Identify hazards and required controls before practical work. Teacher instruction and the current safe operating procedure take priority over website summaries.
