Small Box Project

In this unit you will make the approved small timber box with an optional lid. Confirm with the teacher whether your box will be open or lidded and record the choice. The confirmed high-level construction features are dovetails, a back rebate, a finger joint, a bottom rebate and square glue-up. Lid chamfers apply only if a lid is selected; teacher-supplied or teacher-approved small hinges apply only to a hinged version.

Optional lidded example. Confirm your lid choice and follow the teacher-issued plan for your project.

Throughout the course you will:

  • Use the approved brief and teacher-issued sources to confirm requirements.
  • Inspect timber, calculate stock from the sides, top and bottom, and establish reliable marking references.
  • Explain the purpose and order of the approved joints and rebates.
  • Record dry-fit, correction and square-assembly checks. For a lidded box, record the applicable lid-fit and hinge-fit checks; for an open box, record opening, edge and access checks.
  • Document surface readiness, the authorised finishing process and final quality.
  • Evaluate the finished product with clear evidence and identify improvements.

This website supports theory and evidence only. Follow teacher direction and current workshop documents for all dimensions, settings, tolerances and practical procedures.
